About

Independent views of the world.

Quasiinvestor is an independent publication. It carries no sponsorships, no paid placements, and no obligation to be optimistic.

Essays here follow whatever seems worth understanding: often fintech, blockchain, agritech, and gaming, and often through a Southeast Asian lens, but never exclusively.

The name is honest: nothing here is investment advice, and the author is at best a quasi-investor. Positions, when they exist, are disclosed in the relevant piece.

New essays are published irregularly.
