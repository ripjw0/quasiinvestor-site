About

Independent views of the world.

Quasiinvestor is an independent publication. It carries no sponsorships, no paid placements, and no obligation to be optimistic. Essays here follow whatever seems worth understanding — often fintech, blockchain, agritech, and gaming, and often through a Southeast Asian lens, but never exclusively.

The name is honest: this is not investment advice, and the author is at best a quasi-investor. Positions, when they exist, are disclosed in the relevant piece.

New essays are published irregularly. The best way to follow along is the subscribe link below, or write to hello@quasiinvestor.com.
